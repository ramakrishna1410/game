# Madras Gethu — Story Bible

> *A boxer who once sold one fight comes home to find the whole shore has been sold.*
> ஒரு ஃபைட்டை விற்றவன் ஊருக்கு திரும்பி வர்றான்... முழு கரையையே விற்றுட்டாங்க.

## Theme: "What's not for sale?" — விலைக்கு இல்லாதது எது?

Every main character is defined by what they sold, or refused to sell.

| Character | Sold / refused |
|---|---|
| **Maaran** | Sold one fight (the 2020 state final) |
| **Inspector Ilango** | Sold his badge, long ago |
| **Rajan** | Was handed a win he never earned, and it rotted his life |
| **Kumaran Anna** | Wants to sell the whole shore |
| **The fishermen** | Refuse to sell. The story is on their side |

The final line of the game answers it:
> **Ilango:** இந்த ஊர்ல எல்லாத்துக்கும் விலை இருக்கு மாறா. உனக்கும் இருந்துச்சு.
> *Everything in this town has a price, Maara. You had one too.*
> **Maaran:** இருந்துச்சு. இப்போ இல்ல.
> *It did. Not anymore.*

## Tone

Grief and humour side by side, like North Chennai itself: a funeral with a gaana song,
a fight that ends with someone asking for tea. Fights are loud; the quiet scenes
(Amma's kitchen, the empty gym at night, the jetty) are where the story lives.

Language: Madras bashai. Swearing stays moderate (dei, poda, loosu, naaye, porampokku,
kasmalam). No caste or community slurs, ever.

Subtitles: Tamil script with English underneath by default; options for Tanglish or English only.

## Cast

### Maaran (மாறன்) — the hero, 28
Ex-boxer from the Royapuram Boxing Club, fisherman Velu's son. Six years in Dubai on
construction sites. Quiet, dry humour, never starts a fight, always finishes it.
**Wound:** he threw the 2020 final so Kumaran's people would clear his father's boat
loan. They didn't, and a week later his father was lost at sea in a storm.
**Lie he believes:** Kumaran's men sank the boat, so his father's death is their sin, not his.
**Arc:** running away → owning what he did → standing where his father couldn't.

### Selvi Amma (செல்வி) — his mother, 52
Runs the idli kadai at the harbour gate. Funny, sharp, tired. The first person the gang
leans on. **Secret:** she knows how Velu really died, and has kept it for six years so
her son could live with himself. Tells him in Chapter 8.

### Kayal (கயல்) — his sister, 22
Final-year nursing student. Wants her brother home and not fighting. Practical and brave.
In Chapter 3 she films Settu's men beating a fisherman, and that video becomes the
evidence the whole plot turns on. Her fiancé **Arun** is a junior engineer on the port
project, so the wedding itself sits on the fault line.

### Durai Vaathiyaar (துரை வாத்தியார்) — his old coach, 60s
Runs the Royapuram Boxing Club. Knew Maaran threw the fight and never forgave him:
*"You sold my ring."* Earning him back is the emotional thread of Act 1. His gym is the
game's training space (the Practice Yard) and skill tree.
> கோபத்துல அடிச்சா கை தான் உடையும். புத்தியோட அடி.
> *Hit in anger and only your hand breaks. Hit with your head.*

### Kutty (குட்டி) — his best friend, 28
Auto driver, comic relief, the town's gossip network. Owes money to Settu, which the
game uses to make him **look** like the traitor in Chapter 7. He isn't. Every clue
against him has an innocent explanation revealed in Chapter 9.

### Rajan (ராஜன்) — the man he lost to, 29
Vyasarpadi boxer who won the 2020 final. Everyone knew Maaran went down on purpose;
Rajan's title was a joke, his nationals were a disaster, and he ended up fighting in
Kumaran's underground ring. He hates Maaran for *giving* him the win.
> நீ குடுத்த வெற்றி... என் வாழ்க்கை பூரா தோல்வி டா.
> *The win you gave me... has been a loss my whole life.*
Act 3 mini-boss. Beat him **clean** and he testifies.

### Settu (செட்டு) — harbour enforcer, 40s
Kumaran's man on the shore: protection money (maamool), the rigged fish auction, the
quiet evictions. Swaggering, clever, cruel. In 2020 he was the one at ringside. He calls
Kumaran "anna" and someone else "sir" — the first clue about Ilango. Act 1 boss.

### Karuppu (கருப்பு) — Settu's Thadiyan
Huge, slow, loyal. Chapter 3 mini-boss.

### Vicky (விக்கி) — Kumaran's son, 26
Spoilt, dangerous, runs drugs through the harbour in the fishing boats. Kumaran's weak
spot. Chapter 9 boss.

### Kumaran Anna (குமரன் அண்ணன்) — the politician, 55
Grew up gutting fish on this same shore and clawed his way out. Now standing for MLA
on the "Pudhiya Kasimedu" port expansion. He believes he is saving the next generation:
> நானும் இந்த கரையில மீன் வெட்டினவன் தான். நான் வெளிய வந்தேன். உங்க பசங்களையும் வெளிய கொண்டு வரேன்.
> *I gutted fish on this shore too. I got out. I'm getting your children out too.*
The man everyone thinks is the villain. Chapter 11 boss.

### Inspector Ilango (இளங்கோ) — the real enemy, 45
The calm, helpful cop who "saves" Kayal in Chapter 5 and feeds Maaran information.
In 2020 he was the sub-inspector who arranged the fix (Settu's "sir"). Now he is using
Maaran to tear down Kumaran's people so he can take the port deal for himself.
**Verbal tic:** ends orders with *"சரியா?"* ("clear?"). Players who listen will hear it
in the prologue phone call. Final boss.

## Structure: Prologue + 3 acts, 12 chapters

### Prologue — இறுதிப் போட்டி / The Final (2020)
You play the state final against Rajan and dominate it. Between rounds Settu shows
Maaran a photo of his father's boat on his phone. Round 3: the prompt says
**"Drop your guard."** The game won't let you win: punches slow, the guard drops,
Rajan's hook lands. A week later Velu's boat doesn't come back from a storm, and the
harbour whispers that Settu's men were at the jetty that night. Maaran leaves.
*The game teaches you to lose before it teaches you to win.*

### Act 1 — திரும்பி வந்தான் / He Came Back
1. **காசிமேடு காலை / Kasimedu Morning.** Kutty's auto brings Maaran home. Settu's boys
   are shaking down Amma's kadai. Tutorial fight. Settu calls: he knows about the fix.
2. **வாத்தியார் / The Coach.** The cold welcome at the gym. Earn your place back:
   grabs and counters are taught here.
3. **மீன் ஏலம் / The Fish Auction.** Settu rigs the auction; the fight breaks out
   across ice boxes and crates. Karuppu mini-boss. Kayal films the beating.
4. **செட்டு / Settu.** Settu's men smash Kutty's auto; a chase through the lanes.
   Settu boss. Going down, he sneers: *"You think you're the only one who sold out?"*

### Act 2 — வேட்டை / The Hunt
5. **திருவிழா / The Festival.** Rowdies go after Kayal at the Amman festival because of
   her video. Inspector Ilango "saves" them and wins Maaran's trust.
6. **கிடங்கு / The Godown.** Diesel-smuggling godown on fire. Rescue the fishermen.
   Maaran finds the port papers his father refused to sign.
7. **துரோகி / The Traitor.** A trap, with everything pointing to Kutty. Maaran is beaten
   and thrown off the breakwater.
8. **கடல் / The Sea.** The fishermen pull him out. Amma tells the truth: nobody killed
   Velu. He knew his son had sold the fight for his loan, and he went out into the
   storm warning out of shame, to earn it back himself. Silambam training with an old
   master unlocks the lathi skills.
   > யாரும் உங்க அப்பாவ கொல்லல டா. அவமானத்துல தான் அவரு கடலுக்கு போனாரு.
   > *Nobody killed your father. He went to sea out of shame.*

### Act 3 — கெத்து / Gethu
9. **விக்கி / Vicky.** Vicky's party at an Ennore resort. Vicky boss. Maaran finds proof
   that Ilango set the Chapter 7 trap; Kutty is cleared.
10. **ராஜன் / Rajan.** Underground ring rematch. Win clean (no weapons, no environment)
    and Rajan agrees to testify. Win dirty and he spits at your feet.
11. **பேரணி / The Rally.** Eviction day: bulldozers on the shore, the whole street fights
    back, then the fight through Kumaran's rally to the stage. Kumaran boss. Rajan's
    confession goes out on the stage mic. Ilango takes Kumaran into "custody" to make
    him, and the evidence, disappear.
12. **புயல் / The Storm.** The jetty in a cyclone, in the same kind of storm that took
    Velu. Ilango final boss.

### The final choice
Ilango is beaten, hanging off the jetty over the sea. Both choices cost something.
- **Pull him up.** The evidence survives; the shore is saved. Maaran goes to jail for the
  rally. Epilogue: Amma visits with idlis; Vaathiyaar's gym is full of kids.
- **Let the sea take him.** Revenge, but the evidence dies with him and the port goes
  ahead. Maaran leaves on a fishing boat at dawn.

### Epilogue — Kayal's wedding
Plays out differently for each ending, and for how you fought (see Nermai below).

## Story in the gameplay

- **Nermai (நேர்மை, honesty) meter.** Clean fighting (fists, counters, letting beaten men
  go) raises it. Weapons on the unarmed, finishers on downed men and environment kills
  lower it. It changes what Vaathiyaar and the street say to you, decides whether the
  Rajan rematch can end with his testimony, and changes the epilogue.
- **Amma's idli kadai is the hub.** Save point; eating there heals you.
- **The prologue fixes the tone.** The first boss fight is one you are forced to lose.
- **Kutty is a fair misdirection.** Every clue against him is explained in Chapter 9, so a
  replay shows how you were fooled.
- **Storm bookends.** The prologue ends with the storm that took Velu; the game ends inside one.
